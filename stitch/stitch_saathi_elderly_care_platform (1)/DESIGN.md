# Design System

## Brand & Style

The design style utilizes a tonal spot variant paired with a serene, organic aesthetic. The visual identity evokes balance, clarity, and reliability, drawing inspiration from natural and grounded design principles. The typography utilizes Literata for headlines and Nunito Sans for body and labels, offering a warm yet precise editorial feel.

## Colors

The color palette is anchored by a deep forest green primary (`#4a7c59`), complemented by a muted secondary tone (`#6b6358`) and a warm tertiary accent (`#c4a66a`). Neutral tones (`#4a4e4a`) provide structure and high legibility across all surfaces in light mode.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Literata** for headlines, imparting an editorial and sophisticated character, and **Nunito Sans** for body and labels to ensure maximum legibility and friendliness across digital interfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

A balanced grid system with standard 1.5rem gutters and 2rem outer margins establishes the spatial rhythm. Component spacing utilizes a consistent scale designed to give content ample breathing room while maintaining tight structural relationships.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is achieved primarily through soft tonal layers and subtle, ambient shadows that provide gentle separation without high-contrast visual harshness.

## Shapes

The shape language uses a moderately rounded approach (`roundedness: 2`), featuring a 0.5rem base border radius for interactive elements and containers, scaling up to 1rem for larger cards. This creates a friendly yet polished aesthetic.

## Components

Components leverage the primary and secondary color palette with rounded borders and clear typography. Buttons feature soft corners, inputs maintain clean outlines with focus states in the primary green hue, and cards utilize subtle tonal elevation.
